# Core Research Questions & Operational Indicators — ILJO v1.0

1. **Accessibility:** What is the geographic and court-level distribution of PHI filings across Indonesia?
2. **Affordability & Procedural Barriers:** How frequently are worker claims dismissed on procedural grounds (*niet ontvankelijke verklaard* / NO) vs. decided on the merits?
3. **Timeliness:** What is the median and IQR duration from `registration_date` to `decision_date` across dispute types and court levels?
4. **Representation:** What proportion of workers vs. employers have legal counsel or union representation, and how does representation correlate with procedural dismissal?
5. **Judicial Reasoning:** Which statutory provisions (UU 2/2004, UU 13/2003, UU 6/2023, PP 35/2021, MK decisions, SEMA) are most frequently cited?
6. **Outcomes:** What is the distribution of `Granted`, `Partly granted`, `Rejected`, and `Dismissed on procedural grounds`?
7. **Remedies:** Which remedies (unpaid wages, severance pay, compensation, reinstatement) are ordered?
8. **Appeals:** What proportion of Rights and Termination disputes show identifiable cassation links? (Note: Interest and Inter-union disputes are final at PHI first instance under [UU No. 2 Tahun 2004](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/40452/uu-no-2-tahun-2004).)
9. **Sectoral & Geographic Distribution:** Which industrial sectors and regions appear most frequently?
10. **Execution Gap:** To what extent is post-judgment execution observable in published decisions? (Hypothesis: `Not observable` in >95% of judgment texts.)
