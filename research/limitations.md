# Methodological Limitations & Evidence Boundaries — ILJO v1.0

1. **Publication Completeness Bias:** Not all PHI or Supreme Court decisions are uploaded promptly or uniformly across all 34+ provincial PHI courts on [Direktori Putusan Mahkamah Agung RI](https://putusan3.mahkamahagung.go.id).
2. **Pre-Litigation Selection Bias:** Disputes resolved in bipartite negotiation, Disnaker mediation, or abandoned before filing are invisible in court judgments.
3. **Non-Observability of Execution:** A published judgment ordering severance or reinstatement is **not** evidence that the worker received payment or returned to work. Execution requires separate *aanmaning* / *sita eksekusi* records not routinely linked to judgment PDFs.
4. **Incomplete Case Linkage:** Linking first-instance PHI decisions to Supreme Court cassation records is constrained by inconsistent case-number formatting and missing cross-references.
5. **v1.0 Sandbox Pilot Limitation:** Due to offline sandbox constraints and unverified `robots.txt` access, all quantitative tables in v1.0 use **synthetic test data** (`data/synthetic/phi_synthetic_pilot.csv`) strictly for pipeline validation—not empirical inference.
