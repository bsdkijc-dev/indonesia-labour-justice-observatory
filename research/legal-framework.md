# Indonesian Labour Justice Legal Framework Register — ILJO v1.0

| Instrument | Citation & Scope | Key PHI Relevance | Verified Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UU No. 2 Tahun 2004** | Penyelesaian Perselisihan Hubungan Industrial (effective 14 Jan 2005) | Establishes PHI at District Courts; 4 dispute types; cassation available for Rights & Termination disputes; final at PHI for Interest & Inter-union disputes. | [BPK JDIH](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/40452/uu-no-2-tahun-2004) |
| **UU No. 6 Tahun 2023 & PP No. 35 Tahun 2021** | Penetapan Perppu Cipta Kerja & PKWT/PHK Implementing Regulation | Governs fixed-term contracts (PKWT), outsourcing, termination grounds, and severance/compensation formulas applicable to 2021–2026 disputes. | [Gadjian / UU Ketenagakerjaan](https://www.gadjian.com/uu-ketenagakerjaan) |
| **Putusan MK No. 94/PUU-XXI/2023 & No. 132/PUU-XXIII/2025** | Constitutional Court Review of Pasal 82 UU PPHI | Clarifies 1-year time limit (*daluarsa*) for workers filing termination lawsuits (most recently counted from failed mediation/conciliation minutes). | [MKRI Putusan 94/PUU-XXI/2023](https://www.mkri.id/public/content/persidangan/putusan/putusan_mkri_9665_1709184364.pdf) / [BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/40452/uu-no-2-tahun-2004) |
| **Putusan MK No. 168/PUU-XXI/2023** | Constitutional Court Review of Job Creation Law (Labour Cluster) | Affirms *upah proses* (procedural wages) obligation until final binding judgment (*inkracht*), creating tension with SEMA No. 3/2015. | [UNTAR JSSH](https://journal.untar.ac.id/index.php/JSSH/article/download/37150/22706/113599) |
| **SEMA No. 3 Tahun 2015** | Supreme Court Circular on *Upah Proses* | Historically capped *upah proses* at 6 months; subject to constitutional tension post-MK 168/PUU-XXI/2023. | [UNTAR JSSH](https://journal.untar.ac.id/index.php/JSSH/article/download/37150/22706/113599) |
| **SEMA No. 3 Tahun 2018** | Supreme Court Circular on Civil/PHI Remedies | Reaffirms cassation as the final legal remedy in PHI; *Peninjauan Kembali* (PK) is not available for PHI cases. | [Universitas Darma Agung](https://jurnal.universitasdarmaagung.ac.id/jurnalrectum/article/download/3152/2867) |

*Note: Researchers must preserve the legal version applicable to each case's filing/termination date and never invent statutory citations.*
