# Research Ethics Charter — Indonesia Labour Justice Observatory (ILJO) v1.0

**Research Director:** Cecep Mustafa, Ph.D.  
**Effective Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Status:** Active Governance Standard

## 1. Core Ethical Principles
1. **Data Minimization:** Collect only fields strictly necessary to answer the research question (*"What Do Indonesian Court Decisions Tell Us About Workers' Access to Justice?"*).
2. **Purpose Limitation:** Use judicial records exclusively for empirical research, policy intelligence, and public legal education.
3. **Source Transparency:** Maintain immutable provenance (`source_url`, `source_retrieved_at`, `document_sha256`) for every record.
4. **Privacy Protection:** Publicly accessible court decisions still contain sensitive personal data. Treat all personal identifiers with strict care; never publish raw names, addresses, NIK, or contact details of workers, litigants, lawyers, or staff.
5. **Reproducibility:** All transformations, exclusions, and statistical calculations must be scripted, version-controlled, and auditable.
6. **Human Accountability:** AI-assisted extraction is advisory only and must be marked `ai_extracted` until independently validated.

## 2. Strict Prohibitions
- **No Unauthorized Access:** Never bypass CAPTCHAs, authentication gates, rate limits, or technical access controls.
- **No Personal Targeting:** Never use court decisions to profile, track, or target individual workers, litigants, or employers.
- **No Judge Performance Rankings:** Never publish rankings, scorecards, or evaluative comparisons of individual judges, ad-hoc judges, or court clerks.
- **No Fabricated Evidence:** Never manufacture empirical findings, impute missing values as `"No"` or `0`, or infer judgment execution from a published decision alone.
- **No Automated Legal Advice:** ILJO provides systemic empirical research and plain-language procedural education—never individualized legal advice.
- **No Misrepresentation:** ILJO is an independent research initiative and must never be represented as an official initiative of Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia.
