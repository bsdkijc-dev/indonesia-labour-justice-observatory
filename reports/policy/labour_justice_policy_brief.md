# Policy Brief: Closing the Evidence & Access Gap in Indonesian Industrial Relations Justice

**Author:** Indonesia Labour Justice Observatory (ILJO) — Research Director: Cecep Mustafa, Ph.D.  
**Status:** Infrastructure & Analytical Framework Ready (Empirical Collection Pending Authorized Access)

## 1. Empirical Status vs. Structural Policy Questions
Because automated access to [Direktori Putusan Mahkamah Agung RI](https://putusan3.mahkamahagung.go.id) could not be verified in the offline sandbox, ILJO v1.0 does not manufacture empirical claims. Instead, this brief establishes the **evidence-to-policy translation framework** grounded in verified Indonesian labour law:

1. **Procedural Dismissal & Pre-Litigation Traps:** Under [UU No. 2 Tahun 2004](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/40452/uu-no-2-tahun-2004), workers must complete bipartite negotiation and Disnaker mediation/conciliation before filing at PHI; procedural defects (such as improper claim joinder or missing mediation minutes) trigger *niet ontvankelijke verklaard* (NO) dismissals.
2. **Time-Bar (*Daluarsa*) Uncertainty:** Constitutional Court rulings ([Putusan MK No. 94/PUU-XXI/2023](https://www.mkri.id/public/content/persidangan/putusan/putusan_mkri_9665_1709184364.pdf) and No. 132/PUU-XXIII/2025) reshaped how the 1-year filing deadline under Pasal 82 UU PPHI is counted, creating urgent need for plain-language worker guidance.
3. **The *Upah Proses* Tension:** [UNTAR JSSH analysis](https://journal.untar.ac.id/index.php/JSSH/article/download/37150/22706/113599) highlights the tension between SEMA No. 3 Tahun 2015 (6-month cap) and Putusan MK No. 168/PUU-XXI/2023 (procedural wages until *inkracht*).
4. **The Execution Observability Gap:** Published court decisions rarely document whether severance or reinstatement orders were actually paid or executed—requiring dedicated *aanmaning* / *eksekusi* monitoring rather than inferring compliance from judgments.

## 2. Policy Recommendations (Non-Exhaustive Framework)
- **Standardized Metadata & Open API:** Mahkamah Agung RI and Kemenaker should publish structured, de-identified PHI metadata (docket dates, dispute types, representation flags) to enable transparent empirical monitoring.
- **Execution Tracking Registry:** Link *penetapan eksekusi* / compliance confirmations to published PHI docket numbers.
- **Legal Aid & Plain-Language Guides:** Expand *Posbakum* and union-assisted filing checklists to reduce avoidable procedural dismissals.
